Privacy policy

This site is public. Sign-in is necessary only for new radio transcripts, notifications, F1TV tokens, and settings on all your devices.

Data we collect

  • Your email address from Google sign-in. We use it only to check that you are on the list of invited people.
  • Your F1TV token, if you save one. We keep it with a hash of your email address to show the car positions to you only.
  • Your push subscription, if you turn on notifications. We keep it with your email address to send you session alerts.
  • Your TV delay, favourite driver and favourite team, if you sign in. We keep them with a hash of your email address to use them on all your devices.
  • Your other settings, such as autoplay. These stay in your browser. Live timing requests send the delay to the site.

How we use and share data

We use the data only to run this site. We do not sell or share it. We do not use it for advertising or tracking.

Storage

An Oracle Cloud VM in Sweden hosts the site. It stores the push subscriptions, the F1TV tokens, the settings and the transcripts. Microsoft Azure processes team radio audio for the transcripts in Norway and Sweden.

Removal

Turn off notifications in the settings to delete your push subscription. Ask the site owner to remove your email address from the list or to delete your F1TV token and settings.
